cities15000.tsv.gz
==================

An extract of the GeoNames "cities15000" dataset (populated places with a
population of 15,000 or more), reduced to the columns FBSAT59 needs:

    name <TAB> latitude <TAB> longitude <TAB> country <TAB> population

Rows are sorted by descending population. Two leading "#" comment lines.

Used by src/comms/aprs/citylookup.py to append "near <city>" to positions
in the APRS tab's Plain view, fully offline.

Source : https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/  (cities15000.zip)

columns 2,5,6,9,15 (1-indexed) as name/lat/lon/country/population.
